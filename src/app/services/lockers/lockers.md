# Lockers

Students who are on a study term and have paid their MathSoc fee are eligible to book a locker for the term. **No perishable food may be stored in lockers, as it can attract pests.**

MathSoc offers lockers on a term-by-term basis, first-come, first-served. For **Fall 2026**, locker sign-outs will open on:

- **September 21** for Math students who have paid their MathSoc fee.
- **September 28** for non-Math students who have paid their MathSoc fee.

**The locker sign-out form will be available here soon. Sign-ups will open on the dates listed above.**

## End-of-term locker cleanout

Please empty your locker by the end of the term. At the start of the following term, lockers will be cleared out and reassigned to students.

Any belongings left behind will be bagged, labelled, and stored in the MathSoc Office for **30 days**. You’ll need **photo ID** to pick up your items. After 30 days, unclaimed belongings will be moved to the lost and found.
