Located in MC3038, the MathSoc Office provides various services from board game and textbook rentals to **free candy**.

## Rentals

MathSoc offers rentals for board games, textbooks, calculators, and other equipment, subject to availability.

All rentals must be checked out through the MathSoc Office during posted office hours. Renters are responsible for returning items on time and in the same condition they were received. MathSoc may refuse additional rentals to anyone with overdue, missing, or damaged items.

### Rental Periods and Limits

* **Board games:** Up to 3 board games may be rented at a time for a rental period of 2 weeks.
* **Calculators:** One calculator may be rented at a time for a rental period of 3 days.
* **Textbooks:** Textbooks may be rented for a rental period of 31 days, subject to availability.
* **Equipment and miscellaneous items:** Rental periods and limits may vary depending on the item and intended use.

## Printing

Available in black and white ($0.10/page) as well as colour ($0.30/side).

## Novelties

We sell stationery, novelty items, and calculators at affordable rates.

# For clubs

## Poster requests

In order to put up posters on MathSoc boards, clubs must gain our approval. The approval process is as follows:

1. Ensure that your request is either:
   - 4 standard A4 sized posters OR
   - 2 large sized posters
2. Submit posters in the slot behind the desk labeled **Postings**

Upon approval, MathSoc will put your posters up for you.
